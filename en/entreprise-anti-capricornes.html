House longhorn beetle treatment: identify and eliminate

How to recognise the house longhorn beetle?

  • Oval exit holes of 6 to 10 mm in the roof frame, beams or softwood timbers
  • Frass and sawdust at the foot of the infested timbers
  • Clearly audible gnawing noises, especially at night and in calm weather: house longhorn beetle larvae bore noisily
  • Oval galleries under the surface of the wood, packed with compacted frass

The house longhorn beetle mainly targets softwoods: roof frames in pine, fir or spruce. Maritime pine, found everywhere around the Bassin d'Arcachon, makes this one of the most exposed areas in France.

Is the house longhorn beetle dangerous for a roof frame?

It is the most destructive wood-boring insect for roof frames. Its larva bores through the wood for 3 to 10 years before becoming an adult: all that time, it hollows out the beams from the inside with no visible sign. A heavily infested roof frame can lose its load-bearing capacity, with a real risk of collapse. That gnawing noise you hear is larvae boring right now: every day counts.

Frequently asked questions

How do I recognise a house longhorn beetle infestation?

The house longhorn beetle leaves oval exit holes of 6 to 10 mm in the wood, often with frass around them. In calm weather, you can sometimes hear the larvae gnawing, especially at night. The damage stays invisible for a long time because the larvae bore beneath the surface.

Why is the house longhorn beetle so dangerous for a roof frame?

The house longhorn beetle larva bores galleries through the wood for 3 to 10 years before becoming an adult. All that time, it weakens the structure with no visible sign. A heavily infested roof frame can lose its load-bearing capacity.

I hear noises in my beams, is it the house longhorn beetle?

Very probably the house longhorn beetle: its larvae gnaw loudly, especially at night and in calm weather. That noise means larvae are boring right now. Have it checked quickly: every season of activity spreads the damage.

How do you treat house longhorn beetles?

After the assessment, we cut back the damaged wood, then treat the timber with a deep injection treatment and surface spraying using certified products. The exact method depends on the extent of the infestation.

Does the roof frame need replacing after a house longhorn beetle attack?

Not necessarily. In the vast majority of cases, an injection treatment is enough to eliminate the larvae and protect the wood. Replacing timbers is only considered if their load-bearing capacity is already compromised, which the technical visit allows us to assess.
