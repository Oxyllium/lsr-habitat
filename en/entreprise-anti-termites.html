Termite treatment: detect the problem and protect your home

How do you know if you have termites in your house?

Termites are the most discreet of all wood-boring insects: they eat wood from the inside without leaving any sawdust. The signs to look for:

  • Wood that sounds hollow or flakes into layers: the surface stays intact while the inside is eaten away
  • Mud tubes (earth-covered tunnels) along walls, skirting boards or foundations
  • Swarming of winged insects in spring, often near windows
  • Woodwork that warps or gives way under light pressure

Only a professional examination can confirm a termite infestation: that is exactly what our free quote is based on.

Are termites dangerous? The special case of Gironde

Gironde is one of the most affected departments in France: many towns, including Bordeaux and the Arcachon Bay, are classified by prefectoral decree, and a termite report is mandatory there to sell a property. A colony can number several million individuals and weaken roof frames, floors and woodwork to the point of threatening the stability of the building. The damage progresses in silence: by the time it becomes visible, the infestation is already well established.

Do natural termite treatments work?

No. A termite colony numbers hundreds of thousands of organised, hidden individuals: home remedies and consumer products never reach the colony. Only a professional treatment (injection, certified barrier or bait stations) removes termites from a house for good.

What happens if a termite infestation is left untreated?

The infestation spreads in silence: termites can weaken a roof frame, floors or woodwork to the point of threatening the stability of the building. The longer you wait, the greater the damage and the cost of the repairs.
